Frequently asked questions

How do I log a trip?

Open your group, go to the Trips tab, and enter the odometer reading at the start and end of your drive. SHARIT calculates the distance and cost automatically using your group's price per kilometer.

How is the price per kilometer decided?

When you create a group, you can set a fixed price yourself or let SHARIT suggest one based on your car's fuel efficiency and current fuel prices. You can switch between a fixed price and an auto-suggested one anytime in group settings.

How are costs split?

Every trip is priced automatically and split evenly among that trip's participants. Fixed costs (like insurance or road tax) are split either evenly or by kilometers driven, based on what you choose when adding the cost — and that split is locked in at the time, so it won't change later if someone joins or leaves the group.

Can I edit a trip after logging it?

Yes. Any group member can correct a trip's odometer readings or notes. Edits are visible to the whole group, so nothing changes quietly.

Can a group have more than one owner?

Yes. An owner can promote any member to owner from group settings, and demote a co-owner back to member — a group always keeps at least one owner. If the last owner leaves the group, SHARIT automatically promotes the longest-standing member.

Who can see what inside a group?

Every member of a group can see all trips, costs, and balances for that group — that's what makes the numbers trustworthy. Groups are fully separated from each other: nothing you do in one group is visible in another.

Does SHARIT handle payments between members?

No. SHARIT keeps the ledger of who owes whom, but settling up happens outside the app — however your group prefers.
